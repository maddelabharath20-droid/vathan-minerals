# Minimal Change & Surgical Precision Standard (Agency Agents)
trigger: always_on

## Operational Discipline
1. **Surgical Scope**:
   - Touch only files directly related to the user's objective.
   - Do not reformat entire files or churn unrelated lines.
2. **Preserve Documentation & Context**:
   - Maintain all existing docstrings, legal headers, and proprietorship branding (`Prop : MADDELA VENKATA SUBBAIAH`, `VATHAN MINERALS - MINING`).
   - Keep comments that explain business logic or workarounds.
3. **Revertability**:
   - Write drop-in replacements that are easy to isolate, review, and revert if needed.
